Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour with Main Tour & Travel?

Simply fill in the inquiry form above or contact us directly via WhatsApp at +92 344 5260907. We'll get back to you within minutes with a customized quote and available packages tailored to your needs.

Do you provide visa assistance for all countries?

We provide visa assistance for 50+ countries including UAE, Turkey, Schengen countries, UK, Malaysia, Thailand, and many more. Our expert team handles all documentation with a near-perfect approval success rate.

What is included in your Umrah packages?

Our Umrah packages include return flights, Haram-view hotel in Makkah, hotel in Madinah, all transfers between cities, full ziyarat tours, and 24/7 on-ground support throughout your sacred journey.

Can you customize packages for families or groups?

Absolutely! We specialize in family and group tours. We offer special group discounts, dedicated tour managers, child-friendly itineraries, and can accommodate groups from 5 to 50+ travelers.

How far in advance should I book my trip?

We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks in advance for international trips to secure the best flight prices and hotel availability. For Umrah packages during peak season (Ramadan), booking 3-4 months ahead is advised.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and cash payments at our office in Jhang. Installment plans are also available for select packages — ask us for details.
